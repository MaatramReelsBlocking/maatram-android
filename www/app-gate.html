Maatram Shield

Turn on the Shield so Hard Lock blocks social apps on your whole phone — Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, YouTube, Facebook, X and Reddit won't open at all while a lock is running. WhatsApp is locked too, but you can unlock only WhatsApp during a session.

⚠️ Android says "Restricted setting" or "blocked for your security"? That's a standard notice for any app installed outside the Play Store — Maatram never touches money, banking or passwords; everything stays on your phone. To allow it: Settings → Apps → Maatram → tap ⋮ (three dots, top-right) → Allow restricted settings → come back and turn the Shield on.
